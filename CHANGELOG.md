# DockTape changelog

## 1.2.0

- iCloud Drive storage by default, with local and custom-folder options and synced AI/device preferences.
- Merge recordings and export transcripts as Markdown, plain text, subtitles, or JSON formats.
- Easier AI summaries: transcribe first when needed, or automatically summarize after transcription.
- Smarter imports skip identical recordings and repair changed or corrupted audio in place.
- UX improvements: new cassette icon, clearer progress indicators, improved search and layouts, and removal of the unused menu-bar icon.
- Stability fixes for iCloud loading, playback, file management, device operations, and cancellation.

Known limitations: simultaneous edits across Macs can still cause iCloud conflicts, and recording-list durations remain estimates.
